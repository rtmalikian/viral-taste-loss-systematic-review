## A. Virus scope and the "no other virus" claim

**R4-01 — Blocker — Abstract (Main results); Background, "Etiologies in scope"; Discussion, "Summary of main results" and "Overall completeness and applicability":** The no-other-virus claim shifts meaning between sentences — "no study … met any pool mapping", "none concerns influenza or any other named virus *alone*", "no eligible study", "there is no eligible quantitative evidence base … to summarise" — while the synthesis supports only the narrower statements that no *included* study is coded influenza and none mapped to a pool, so the four wordings must be harmonised to that narrower claim everywhere.

**R4-02 — Blocker — Abstract and Discussion statements of R4-01, read against Methods (Search methods), Results (Study flow) and Discussion (Potential biases):** An absolute absence claim is not sustainable from this search — Embase, Scopus, Web of Science, CINAHL and CENTRAL were not run, the WHO ICTRP search was not run, 226 main-pool plus 19 citation-chasing reports were never retrieved in full text, and 99 records remain awaiting classification — so every statement of the finding must carry the qualifier "in the searches run and records retrieved/classified" at the point of claim, not only in a limitations paragraph.

**R4-03 — Major — Plain Language Summary (opening and pathogenesis paragraphs):** The PLS — the text most likely to be quoted — generalises where the body does not: it opens with generic "when people catch a virus", says only "almost all" of 389 studies were about COVID-19 (the body count is 385 SARS-CoV-2 plus 4 unspecified possible viral, with no influenza or other named-virus study at all), and its pathogenesis paragraph speaks of "the virus" infecting taste buds with no SARS-CoV-2 qualifier, so the PLS must state plainly that all pooled and all Q2 evidence is SARS-CoV-2 and that no flu/other-virus evidence was found.

**R4-04 — Major — Q2 Results and Abstract (pathogenesis sentences), read against Summary of Findings downgrade reasons:** Indirectness is stated plainly and downgraded for the Q1/Q3 GRADE rows ("all SARS-CoV-2 evidence read against a question about viral infections generally") but the Q2 verdicts carry no equivalent certainty or indirectness statement — the Abstract's pathogenesis sentences name no virus at all — so a reader can take the mechanism ranking as a general viral ranking when the synthesis (§2.1) states every pathogenesis statement is, on the included evidence, a statement about SARS-CoV-2 only.

**R4-05 — Minor — Abstract (Background) and Background, "The complaint, as it presents in family medicine":** General pre-COVID claims — taste loss "is a common complaint during and after viral infections" and taste disturbance "was a recognised accompaniment of upper respiratory infections" in 2019 — are asserted with no citation, contrary to the protocol's Background citation standard (every Background claim traces to a verified citation actually retrieved and read), and they pre-load the general-viral framing the Results then have to retract.

**R4-06 — Minor — Discussion, "Overall completeness" ("the search was built to find influenza and other-virus studies"), against Appendix A strings:** The influenza term is explicit in both run strings, but capture of other named viruses depends on the generic terms viral/virus/coronavirus/"upper respiratory" appearing in title/abstract, so a record naming a virus without those generic words would be missed and the "built to find other-virus studies" claim needs that qualification.

## B. Protocol consistency of the Q2 evidence base

**R4-07 — Blocker — Results, Q2 and Table 3, against Protocol (Eligibility) and Methods, "Differences between protocol and review":** Six narrative/bibliographic reviews that the extraction form flags "do not extract as primary" contribute 16 theory-code assignments to the Q2 counts (synthesis §8, problem 5), although the locked protocol excludes narrative reviews from synthesis and retains them for citation chasing only; their retention is not declared among the six protocol differences, no primary-evidence sensitivity count excluding them is given, and the Discussion's assurance that "the Q2 conclusions do not rest on them" is asserted, not shown — for T7 and T6 one of only three mixed-grade carriers each is such a review (R00592).

**R4-08 — Minor — Background, "How taste could be lost", and Methods, "Differences", against Protocol (Q2 taxonomy):** The locked protocol grades evidence on five grades (direct-human / indirect-human / animal / in-vitro / speculative), but the manuscript and the settled data use a sixth grade, "mixed as stated" (14 studies, 39 assignments, undecomposable per synthesis §8, problem 3), and this deviation is nowhere declared as a protocol difference.

## C. Taxonomy plausibility and internal consistency (T1–T11)

**R4-09 — Major — Background, "How taste could be lost" (taxonomy as presented for a general viral question):** The taxonomy is covertly SARS-CoV-2-shaped — T1 is defined around ACE2/TMPRSS2-type viral-entry-factor expression, an entry route influenza and most other respiratory viruses do not use — yet neither the taxonomy presentation nor the Q2 conclusions state that plausibility rankings built on entry-factor biology cannot transfer across viruses with different receptors and tissue tropism.

**R4-10 — Major — Background taxonomy list and Results, Q2 (treatment of T4):** T4 is ranked alongside tissue mechanisms as if commensurable, but the synthesis (§3, T4) states explicitly that T4 "is a claim about measurement and attribution, not a tissue mechanism" competing only at the level of what a symptom report means, and the manuscript never gives the reader that distinction, so "the theory with the most coherent direct evidence" reads as a pathogenesis result rather than a finding about outcome misclassification.

**R4-11 — Major — Table 3 and Results, Q2 (directness note):** The manuscript notes that directness is a study-level grade propagated to every code and illustrates it with T8, but omits the scale the synthesis reports — 108 of 223 Q2 studies (48.4%) carry two or more codes (synthesis §1) — so every per-theory direct-human count in Table 3 is an upper bound and must be labelled as such with the multi-code proportion stated beside the table.

**R4-12 — Major — Results, Q1 frequency and Q2 virus composition (SARS-CoV-2 treated as one entity):** SARS-CoV-2 is pooled and discussed as a monolith — the only variant mention in the body is "variant-qualified records" — with no variant- or era-stratified estimate or sensitivity analysis, although variant-qualified and Omicron-specific studies are in the included set and the frequency pool's heterogeneity (I² 99.5%, prediction interval 5.3 to 86.7%) is reported as surviving subgrouping by ascertainment, setting and risk of bias, none of which is the virological variable most likely to explain it.

**R4-13 — Minor — Background taxonomy list (T2 label):** "Sustentacular" is olfactory-epithelium terminology imported into the taste taxonomy without comment, and the synthesis (§5) shows T2's support *is* that olfactory analogy in the source papers' own wording, so presenting the label as settled taste anatomy in the taxonomy list overstates the construct before the Results qualify it.

**R4-14 — Minor — Table 3 / Results, Q2 (T11):** T11 is defined as "Other (specify)", yet the synthesis (§8, problem 6) records that 9 of its 28 studies carry no specification at all in the settled field, so for nearly a third of the residual category the mechanism cannot even be named — a fact the manuscript never discloses while listing T11 counts beside the specified theories.

## D. Theory-by-theory verdicts versus the synthesis file

**R4-15 — Major — Abstract (Main results), Discussion (Summary of main results), and Results, Q2 opening verdict paragraph ("three theories have direct human evidence … in a coherent way"):** This ranking does not match the synthesis (§4), which gives the raw direct-human ranking as T11 = 5, T5 = 4, T1 = 3 and T4 = 3 tied, names T9's single imaging study as meaningful direct evidence alongside T4 and T5, and reports its three best-supported theories (T5, T4, T1) only "on coherence-adjusted grounds" with the raw-count leader T11 named alongside — the manuscript drops both the T11 raw lead and T9 from its headline ranking, then contradicts itself by later crediting T9 with "one genuinely direct imaging study".

**R4-16 — Major — Results, Q2 (T1 paragraph) and Abstract ("direct infection of taste tissue … one null direct study among three"):** No direct-human T1 study demonstrated infection of human taste buds — R00058 sampled nasal/pharyngeal tissue and was null, R00840 is a nasopharyngeal ACE2 genetic association, and R01523 measured taste thresholds — so labelling the trio "direct infection of taste tissue" overstates directness; the synthesis is careful that T1's most taste-bud-specific support is in-vitro (R00710) and animal expression work, and the manuscript must say plainly that human in-vivo taste-bud infection was not shown in any included study.

**R4-17 — Major — Results, Q2 (T5 paragraph):** T5 is presented as taste-mechanism evidence without the synthesis's central caveat (§3, T5) that three of its four direct-human studies were measured against composite smell-and-taste or dysosmia/dysgeusia outcomes (R00084, R01294, R00066) rather than taste-specific outcomes — the manuscript does concede the taste-bud-turnover half of the label is unmeasured, but not that the inflammation-association half is largely not taste-specific either.

**R4-18 — Major — Results, Q2 (T4 paragraph):** The T4 evidence is mis-grouped in three ways against the synthesis (§3, T4): C4367 was a survey of whether respondents could differentiate basic tastes, not an objective-testing study, yet it is counted among "purpose-built objective-testing studies"; the 23.5% objectively hypogeusic figure (R00232) comes from a mixed-as-stated study, not from "the same studies" as the three direct ones; and C11888's taste-strip result rests on 30 tested participants, a denominator the manuscript omits while quoting 86.7 to 96.7% per taste quality.

**R4-19 — Major — Table 3 (T9 row: 2 direct-human) against Results, Q2 (T9 sentence):** The text accounts for only one of T9's two direct-human studies (R00185, diffusion MRI) and never mentions the second, R01073 (decreased vividness of taste mental imagery, patient-reported, per synthesis §3, T9), leaving the table and the narrative irreconcilable on the theory the Abstract's ranking also omits (see R4-15).

**R4-20 — Minor — Results, Q2 (six-theories paragraph, T8):** T8's sole indirect support is quoted as "one indirect D-dimer association (R01957)" with no magnitude, whereas the synthesis (§3, T8) prints means of 0.54 ± 0.32 versus 0.49 ± 0.51 mg/L FEU — a trivial, widely overlapping difference in a non-specific marker — and without the numbers the indirect grade reads as materially stronger than the underlying datum.

**R4-21 — Minor — Results, Q2 (six-theories paragraph, T2 and T3 entries):** The per-theory thumbnail summaries are incomplete against the synthesis: T2's entry omits its one indirect-human study (R02077, oral mucosal epithelial inflammation/damage) and T3's omits the animal-graded R02218 from its non-speculative support, so two of the six "inference only" verdicts are argued from a subset of their own tallies.

**R4-22 — Nit — Abstract (Main results, pathogenesis sentence):** "73.5% of the grades were speculative" misstates the unit — directness is a study-level grade and 73.5% is the share of the 223 Q2 *studies* graded speculative, as the Results text itself correctly puts it.

**R4-23 — Nit — Background, "The complaint" ("SARS-CoV-2 produced taste complaints at a scale no previous virus had generated in the literature"):** This superlative comparative claim about all previous viruses is not a finding of this search (which was not designed to compare historical scale across viruses) and should be softened or supported.

**R4-24 — Nit — Results, Q2 (T11 paragraph):** Two of T11's five "unrelated" direct studies (R00044, R00066) are the same studies already counted as direct evidence for T5, and the paragraph does not note the overlap, so a reader summing direct studies across theories will double-count them.
