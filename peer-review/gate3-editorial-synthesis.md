## Consolidated findings (deduplicated; overlaps merged)

**Blockers (8 unique):**
- C-B1 Duplicate paper in the included count: R01101 = R02974 (Ohla 2022), undisclosed (R6-B1; also R7-m7).
- C-B2 Abstract/PLS/Discussion state subgroup differences as findings with no interaction test (R2-B1, R6-M4, R2-M1).
