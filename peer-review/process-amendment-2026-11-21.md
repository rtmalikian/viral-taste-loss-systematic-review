# Process amendment — peer review as a loop, not a gate (2026-11-21)

Author direction (Raphael Malikian, 2026-11-21 02:16 PT): "in the real world the reviewers feedback would be implemented so make the reviewers feedback less of a gate and more of a loop."

Standing rule from this date, matching the original Gates protocol: reviewer feedback is implemented in revision loops — fix → re-review by the original reviewers → fix again — until no Blocker and no Major remains open. A point is escalated to the author only after 3 loops with that point still open, stated plainly. Items the loop can resolve methodologically are not held as "author actions". Genuine author-level choices (strategy, framing, eligibility calls the author has reserved) still go to him.

Phase 6 of the viral taste-loss review was run under this rule; its record is manuscript/peer-review/loop-report.md.
