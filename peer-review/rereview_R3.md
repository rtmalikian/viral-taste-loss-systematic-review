## Blockers

- **R3-01 — FIXED** — The net-overstatement claim is gone (the word "overstates" no longer appears). Discussion, "Overall completeness and applicability" now reads that self-report "can fold flavour loss into a taste report — although the net direction at population level is not simple, since the psychophysical subgroup's point estimate sits above the self-report subgroup's (47.4% versus 36.5%, descriptive only), the opposite of what a simple misattribution story predicts"; the "Agreements and disagreements" paragraph makes the same point consistently.
- **R3-02 — FIXED** — Results, Q2 T4 paragraph now reads "supported by two purpose-built objective-testing studies and one survey": C11888 is given its denominator ("among the 30 participants tested", normal in 86.7–96.7% per taste quality) and C4367 is correctly described as "a prospective survey in which 60% of respondents could still differentiate the basic tastes at first survey, rising to 88.9% at follow-up — self-differentiation, not a psychophysical test." The Plain language summary no longer groups the three as objective-testing studies, and the R00232 counterweight (23.5% objectively hypogeusic) is retained, so T4 is not rewritten as "all reported loss is smell loss."

## Majors

- **R3-03 — FIXED** — Results, Q2 T5 paragraph now states that three of the four direct-human studies (R00084, R01294, R00066) "were measured against composite smell-and-taste or dysosmia/dysgeusia outcomes rather than taste-specific outcomes, so T5's taste-specific directness is weaker than its count suggests; and the taste-bud-turnover half of the theory's label is not measured in any included human study." The Abstract no longer asserts taste-specific directness for T5, ranking it only "on coherence grounds."
- **R3-04 — FIXED** — Background taxonomy now carries the category caveat verbatim in substance: "T4 is not a tissue mechanism: it is a claim about measurement and attribution, about what a symptom report means, and it competes with the tissue theories only at that level."
- **R3-05 — FIXED** — The trajectory phrasing ("most people's taste recovered completely over the following weeks to months") is deleted. "Implications for practice" now reads "in the follow-up studies most participants had recovered completely at whichever check point their study used, from within 4 weeks to beyond 12 weeks (pooled complete recovery 85.2 to 87.8% across the three windows, each from a different set of studies, very low certainty)"; the Plain language summary likewise adds "Those check points come from different groups of studies, not from following the same people over time."
- **R3-06 — PARTIALLY FIXED** — The gap is now declared: Methods Difference 9 states the recovery-by-ascertainment subgroup was not run because "ascertainment was not carried as a separable variable in the recovery analysis set," and the GRADE footnotes note taste and recovery are "predominantly self-reported without validated instruments." - **R3-07 — FIXED** — Discussion, "Overall completeness and applicability" now bounds the estimates by age: "The pooled estimates derive overwhelmingly from adult cohorts; no age-stratified or paediatric-separable estimate was produced (the protocol's age subgroup was not run: Difference 9), and the figures should not be read as applying to children."
- **R3-08 — PARTIALLY FIXED** — Results, Q3 final paragraph is now a full special-populations account: children (adolescent series R00782, ages 12–17, no taste-specific outcome), cerebrolysin eligibility ages 10–70 with no posted results (R03352), pregnancy as an exclusion in both trials reporting exclusions (C9634, R00627), the single older-adult report (pregabalin case at age 87, R01519), diabetes (exclusion in the prednisolone trial; dosed subgroup in the coffee study), immunocompromise appearing only as exclusions, polypharmacy only as an interaction caution, no data for chronic kidney/liver disease or pre-existing chemosensory disorders, no dose adjustment for any special population, and the R00334 finding that only respondents under 40 rated steroids/smell training more than slightly effective. Residuals: (1) still no Q1/Q2 applicability statement for the pregnant-women cohort in the included set (R00129 appears only in the reference list and characteristics table, as a composite smell-and-taste study, N=13); (2) the Discussion still does not return to special populations beyond the paediatric bound in R3-07 — the expansion lives in Results only.
